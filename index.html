    Treat this like a ritual. Pick your portal: a scarf, ribbon or strip of tape on the floor, or a doorway in your home or office you walk through with intention. Both timelines exist for you. The decisions you make take you onto one.

    1. Stand in front of your portal, in the timeline you're in right now. Feel your feet on the ground.

    3. Say your why out loud. No rehearsing. "I'm building this..."

    5. Notice how it feels in your body. That's her.

    Your why prompt 
    I just spoke a voice note about why I'm building my business and the life I'm creating. Please arrange my words into something I can read back to myself every day.

- Use my words. Don't add new ideas and don't swap my phrases for fancier ones. It should still sound like me talking.
- Take out filler, repeats and false starts (um, like, you know, I mean).

- Put it in an order that flows: why I want this life, why this business, the life I'm building, and how it feels to live it.
- Short paragraphs. No headings, no bullet points, no dashes.
- Keep it under 400 words.
- Give me only the arranged text, with nothing before or after it.

    EFT tapping

    Clear the wobble

    If your throat, chest or belly tightened while you said your desires, this is for you. That wobble is just the old story trying to keep you small. We're going to tap it out, so all that's left is her.

    What is tapping?

    EFT tapping is a simple way to calm your nervous system while you speak to what's coming up. You tap lightly on points on your face and body while you say the words out loud. Your body gets the message that it's safe, and the old feeling loses its grip.

    Use two or three fingertips and tap gently, about five to seven times on each point. You can use one hand or both. There's no wrong way to do it.

    Where to tap

    • Karate chopThe fleshy outer edge of your hand, below your pinky. You tap here for the setup.
    • EyebrowWhere your eyebrow begins, right by the bridge of your nose.
    • Side of eyeOn the bone at the outer corner of your eye.
    • Under eyeOn the bone under your eye, about an inch below your pupil.
    • Under noseThe small spot between your nose and your upper lip.
    • ChinThe crease between your bottom lip and your chin.
    • CollarboneJust below where your collarbones meet, about an inch out to each side.
    • Under armOn your side, about four inches below your armpit. Around your bra line.
    • Top of headThe crown, right at the top of your head.
